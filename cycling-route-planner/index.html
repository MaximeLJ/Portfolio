Routecard

Bike routing that reads the road, not just the distance.

Imported track shown on the map as a reference. Use it to set your start/finish, or turn it into an editable route.

Turns the track into stops that BRouter will re-route through on real roads — retracing it as closely as it can, and rerouting around anything you mark to avoid.

Each pin bulges out the nearest stretch of your route (the segment between two consecutive stops, start, or finish) rather than spurring off one point — drag to reposition. Once you've plotted a route, drag the "extend" slider and watch the real distance update live instead of guessing a number. Works whether you're looping or riding point-to-point.

Tap the map to mark — keep tapping to add more. Click the button again to stop.

Click points tracing along just that road — not a parallel path. Then finish it.

Save your marked areas and banned roads once, then load them into any future route.
